# Code of Conduct for SelectDistinctAU

## Our Pledge

We believe in building a community where everyone feels welcome, respected, and heard — regardless of who they are or where they come from.

We believe in people. In peace. In respect. In discussion. In consensus.

We build tools to help. We listen, consider and reflect. We match our speaking to the listener. We act with care.

## Standards

Trust is the baseline. This community depends on people showing up in ways that keep space for others to do the same. When that trust is eroded, engagement stalls.

The following patterns are incompatible with maintaining that space:

| Pattern | What it looks like | Why it matters |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Bullying or intimidation | Repeated targeting, condescension, or power plays | Turns collaboration into combat |
| Derailment | Steering discussions away from the topic despite gentle redirection | Wastes collective attention |
| Hurtful remarks in jest | "Just joking" used to dismiss concern about harm | Forces the recipient to absorb the cost |

This isn't exhaustive — it's meant as a signal of what we won't tolerate. If something feels off to you, it probably is. Say something anyway.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The maintainers of this organization are responsible for clarifying and upholding our standards of kindness, inclusion, and constructive dialogue. We will take fair and thoughtful action to support a positive environment for all.

The maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not reflect these values — and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Enforcement Ladder

We respond to concerns proportionally and transparently. When harm occurs, maintainers will:

| Level | Action | When |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Correction | Private note explaining why the behaviour doesn't fit | First instance, good faith |
| Warning | Public record of the concern; no repeat allowed | Repeat or more serious impact |
| Restriction | Temporary removal from discussion spaces | Continued pattern or significant harm |
| Removal | Permanent departure from the community | Unaddressed harm or threat to safety |

Maintainers document incidents internally, share summaries with affected parties, and keep records secure. Anyone subject to enforcement can request review by a designated neutral party outside the original decision-maker.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include using an official e-mail address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

Reports can be submitted at: https://selectdistinct.au/CodeOfConductReport

Submissions are confidential and seen only by designated reviewers. We aim to acknowledge every report within 48 hours. Name and email fields are optional — you may report anonymously by leaving them blank.

We are deeply disappointed if harm continues because no one spoke up. Creative and cognitive work requires safety. When people feel threatened or vulnerable, they cannot bring their best selves, their ingenuity, or their inventiveness to the table. We need to know if that safety is being compromised, so we can address it appropriately.

We are here to listen, to help, and to make sure this space stays safe for everyone.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
